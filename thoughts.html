Every Christian parent and teacher wants to do more than transfer knowledge to the precious souls that have been entrusted to them. We want to give our children a foundation upon which they can securely and confidently build their lives.

RI was born out of a desire to provide homeschooling families with excellent, educational resources that seek to shape the student's affections in way that accords with the nature of God, as revealed in the Bible. (Deut 6:6-7)
