Knowing who God is and who we are is the foundation of all other knowledge. In this course, we will spend the first semester exploring what the Bible teaches us about God’s nature, His character, and the ultimate purposes of life. In the second semester, we will delve into the nature of humanity, examining how we are created in God’s image, the purpose of our creation, the reality of our fallenness, and the redemption that restores us to our original purpose.

Remember, these courses are designed for you to collaborate with other homeschool families who share similar educational vision.
