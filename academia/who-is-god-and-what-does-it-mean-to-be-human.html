Who is God, and What does it mean to be human

Knowing who God is and who we are is the foundation of all other knowledge. In this course, we will spend the first semester exploring what the Bible teaches us about God’s nature, His character, and the ultimate purposes of life. In the second semester, we will delve into the nature of humanity, examining how we are created in God’s image, the purpose of our creation, the reality of our fallenness, and the Redemption that restore us to our original purpose.

Course Curriculum

Who is God (Semester 1)

  • There is only one true God.
  • God is a spirit (God is an "I" and not a thing).
  • The Personhood of God (Trinity).
  • The ultimate ends for which God created.
  • The excellencies of God in respect to His essence.
  • The excellencies of in respect to His character.

What does it mean to be human (Semester 2)?

  • The purpose for which God created mankind.
  • Created in His image.
  • What it means to be fallen.
  • Redemption and restoration.
  • How foundational human institutions reflect our being created in God’s image.
